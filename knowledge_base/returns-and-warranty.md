# Returns and warranty

## Returns
All orders go through Amazon, so returns do too. The customer starts a return in Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon sends the return label and handles the refund.
We cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund from our side. Explaining how a return, a refund, or the warranty works is an answer we can give. When the customer asks us to refund them, to take an item back, or to chase a refund, a colleague answers.

## Warranty
- Paddle boards: 2 years on the board, the seams, and the valve. 1 year on the paddle, pump, fin, and leash.
- Shelving: 2 years on panels and hardware.
The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover damage from use, such as a puncture from a rock.

## Other products
For any other Timber & Tide product, the same rules apply: returns through Amazon within 30 days, and a 2-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
